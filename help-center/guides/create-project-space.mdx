## Quick Start

Set up your first project in Pau Vilar's platform.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log In">
    Visit `https://pauvilardocs.com` and log in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a Project">
    Click "New Project" on your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name Your Space">
    Enter your project name and description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Collaborators">
    Add team emails to share the space instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>
