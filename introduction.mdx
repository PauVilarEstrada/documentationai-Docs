## Key areas covered

You can tailor the content to match your product, but most documentation sets benefit from a few consistent areas:

- Product overview and positioning
- Setup and first-use guidance
- Core features and concepts
- Operational notes and maintenance
- Changes, releases, and next steps

<Callout kind="tip">
Keep each section short enough that a reader can scan it in a few seconds. If a topic needs depth, give it its own page.
</Callout>
